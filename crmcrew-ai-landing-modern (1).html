Before you book

Is this secretly a sales call? +
We're a business, so yes — if we can genuinely help, we'll tell you how working with us would look. But the audit itself is real work with real output, and there's zero pressure to go further. Plenty of people take the shortlist and run with it themselves. That's fine by us.
Do I need to be technical? +
Not at all. You need to know your business — where the time goes, what frustrates your team, where things fall through the cracks. We translate that into what's buildable. No jargon required on your side.
What do I need to prepare? +
Nothing formal. Come with a rough idea of the processes that eat the most time or cause the most errors. If you've got numbers — volumes, headcount on a task, hours spent — even better, but we can work without them.
What kind of businesses is this for? +
Established businesses with real operational processes — typically teams of five to a hundred. If you're doing repetitive work in sales, quoting, onboarding, fulfilment or admin, there's almost certainly something worth automating.
Why is it free? +
Because it's the best way for both of us to find out if there's a fit, and because some percentage of the people we help for free become clients. That's the whole model. No tricks, no "limited time" nonsense.
